# Retail Sales Performance & Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Looker Studio dashboard** designed to analyse retail store sales, revenue, costs, product performance, and regional sales trends.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing store sales data and presenting key business metrics through interactive visualisations. The dashboard helps understand sales performance across different states, cities, product categories, and order types.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Looker Studio** – Dashboard development and data visualisation
- **Data Analysis** – Sales performance and business insights
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, tables, KPI cards, and geographical maps

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- **Sales KPIs:** Total Sales, Cost, Quantity, Maximum Sales, and Transactions.
- **Regional Analysis:** State-wise and geographical sales performance.
- **Product Analysis:** Product category contribution and quantity sold.
- **Monthly Trends:** Monthly sales performance and comparison.
- **Interactive Filters:** Customer, category, state, city, and order type.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Uttar Pradesh recorded the highest sales among the displayed states.
- West Bengal ranked second in state-wise sales.
- Single Monk was the leading product category by displayed contribution.
- The dashboard provides an overview of sales distribution across regions and product categories.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To transform store sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, helping users monitor performance, identify trends, and support data-driven business decisions.
